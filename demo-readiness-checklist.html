Talking Points (Q&A)

Q: How accurate is the URL detection?

Our XGBoost model trained on 10,000+ phishing URLs achieves 85-92% accuracy. In production, we'd combine this with real-time threat feeds. We prioritize catching real threats over false positives.

Q: Can this work offline?

The extension can display cached results, but new analyses require the backend. For true offline support, we'd embed smaller models directly in the extension, but that adds 40MB. For networks, our approach works well.

Q: What was your biggest challenge?

Getting explainability right. Anyone can classify something as dangerous, but helping users UNDERSTAND why was hard. We balanced technical accuracy with plain language explanations.

Troubleshooting

Backend won't start

Kill the process using port 8000:

netstat -ano | findstr :8000

Then: taskkill /PID <PID> /F

Frontend won't connect to backend

Verify backend is running: curl http://127.0.0.1:8000/

Analysis taking too long

Try a different URL. Have 2-3 backup URLs ready.
